Privacy policy

You stay in control of your files.

Last updated: 1 October 2026

CleanFerah and contact

This policy covers the CleanFerah app for iPhone and iPad (published by Burak Uluzmanoğlu) and the CleanFerah Android app. Privacy and support contact: uluzman47@gmail.com.

On-device processing

File scanning, exact duplicate and similar-photo analysis, previews and vault operations run on your device. CleanFerah does not upload your files to an app server. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and no account is required. The app does not collect data about you.

Permissions

Photos access lets the app scan the photos and videos you allow; with limited access only the items you select are scanned. Files are read only from folders you add with the Files picker. Content stored only in iCloud is not downloaded automatically. You can revoke access in Settings. The vault opens with Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode; CleanFerah never receives face, fingerprint or passcode data.

Local records and retention

Settings, links to authorized folders, protection preferences and cleanup history are stored in app storage. History may include a file name, location, size, operation result and time. You can clear history in the app and forget saved folder access. These records may be included in your device’s own backup.

Encrypted vault and backup

Vault files and metadata, including names, are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is kept in the Keychain, bound to this device and protected by user authentication. The vault is excluded from iCloud and device backups and locks when the app goes to the background. Deleting the app, resetting the device or removing the device passcode can permanently lose access to the vault; export important files first.

Deletion and export

Files are removed only after your selection and the relevant confirmations. Photos move to the Recently Deleted album in iOS; files in folders you selected are deleted permanently after a separate final confirmation. Deleting a file stored with a cloud provider may synchronize to your other devices. Copying into the vault does not delete the original. Exported copies are unencrypted outside the vault.

Other apps and support

Safari history, app hiding and Hidden album guides link to Apple support pages; those pages and the email app you open apply their own privacy policies. If you email support, your address, message and attachments are processed only to answer your request. Do not attach sensitive files. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the same address.

Your choices and updates

Manage permissions in Settings, clear history in the app and remove vault files through the verified export flow. Contact support with privacy questions. Policy updates will appear on this page with an updated date.
